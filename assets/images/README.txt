SAMPLE PHOTO ASSETS
===================

The JPG files in this folder are local sample photographs used by the opening collage, gallery, story timeline, date vignette, and favorite-photo page.

Replace them with your personal photos while keeping the same filenames, or update the relative paths in index.html and assets/js/script.js. Use landscape or portrait images around 1200px on the long edge for crisp display without oversized downloads. Keep originals and personal photos local to this folder if you do not want to publish them elsewhere.

The grayscale and warm-film looks are CSS treatments. The image files themselves are normal JPEGs.
