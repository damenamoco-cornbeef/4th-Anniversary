Happy 4th Anniversary sa atoa, camel ❤️ Grabe, 4 years na diay ta, bab.❤️ Murag dili gihapon ko katuo nga nakaabot ta ani kadugaya. Daghan na kaayo tag naagian, daghan og challenges, misunderstandings, away, tampuhan, ug times nga dili ta magkasinabot, pero here we are, still choosing each other. And for me, that’s one of the most beautiful things about our relationship. Despite everything, ikaw gihapon akong pilion, every single day.

Happy 4 years of loving each other, bab. Thank you for staying with me, for understanding me bisan usahay lisod ko sabton, and for loving me even on the days nga dili ko okay. Thank you for being part of my life and for giving me a kind of love nga worth fighting for. I know dili perfect atong relationship, and dili pud perfect ang distance nga naa sa tunga nato, pero I hope you know nga seryoso jud ko sa atoa. Dili lang ni love karon, dili lang ni tungod kay happy ta. I want you even on the hard days, even when things get complicated, and even when the distance makes everything feel so difficult. ❤️

LDR is never easy, bab. There are days nga gusto na lang tika i-hug, i-kiss, ug kuyogan without worrying about the distance. Kanang gusto ra ko nga naa ka sa akong kilid, makigchika nimo before matulog, makakita sa imong smile in person, ug makahimo og simple memories with you. Usahay sakit hunahunaon nga through screen ra sa ta magtapad, nga dili tika ma-comfort dayon kung mingawon ka or kung naa kay problema. But even if we're miles apart, I hope you always feel nga naa ra ko diri, loving you in every way I can. Someday, dili na nato kinahanglan mag-goodbye after every call, dili na ta maghulat og months or days para magkita, and we can finally enjoy the little things nga karon atong gina-dream lang. I pray nga maabot ta ana nga time, bab.

I know naa koy mga flaws. Usahay overthinker ko, usahay dali ko masakitan, usahay mahimong emotional ko, and sometimes dili nako ma-explain tarong akong gibati. I'm sorry for the times nga nasakitan tika, sa akong mga words nga dili unta nako angay masulti, and sa mga moments nga instead nga magkaintindihan ta, mag-away nuon ta. I'm still learning how to love you better, how to understand you more, and how to become a better partner for you. I don't promise nga dili na ta mag-away, but I promise nga I will keep trying to understand you and work things out with you, as long as we're both willing to fight for us. Dili man nato malikayan ang problems, pero I hope dili nato kalimtan nga kita gihapon ang magkauban nga moatubang ana.❤️

Bab, if there's one thing I want you to remember, it's that you are so special to me. Dili lang ikaw akong uyab, ikaw pud akong best friend, akong comfort person, akong pahulay sa kapoy nga adlaw, ug usa sa mga rason nganong naa koy something to look forward to every day. You make me happy in ways nga usahay dili nako ma-explain. Even the smallest things you do can make my day better. Your messages, your voice, your presence bisan pinaagi lang sa screen, and the little moments we share mean so much to me. I hope I make you feel loved the same way you make me feel loved. ❤️

Four years down, and I still want more years with you. More birthdays together, more anniversaries, more random calls, more late-night conversations, more silly arguments nga mauwi ra sa panag-uli, more memories, and hopefully, more moments nga magkauban na ta in person. I want to see you grow, achieve your dreams, and become the person you want to be. I want to support you when life gets hard, celebrate with you when things go right, and remind you nga naa kay someone nga proud kaayo nimo.

I'm not asking for a perfect love story, bab. I just want a real one with you. A love nga dili dali mo-give up, a love nga willing maminaw, mo-adjust, mo-sabot, ug mo-grow together. I want us to keep choosing each other, not just because we've been together for four years, but because we still see a future worth building together.

Thank you for being my person, bab. Thank you for these four years of love, lessons, patience, happiness, and memories. If I could go back to the beginning, knowing all the challenges we would face, I would still want to meet you and experience this love with you. Because despite the distance, the hard days, and everything we've been through, loving you is still something I am grateful for.

Happy 4th anniversary sa atoa, my camella home! 🥹💗 I love you so much, more than these words could ever explain. I hope one day, makabawi ta sa tanan nga moments nga dili ta magkauban. I hope maabot ang time nga ako na mismo ang mo-hug nimo, mo-hold sa imong hand, mo-kiss nimo, and tell you how much I love you without a screen between us.

Until that day comes, please take care of yourself for me, okay? Ayaw kalimot og kaon sa saktong oras, pagpahulay kung kapoy na, and always remember nga naa ra ko diri para nimo. Even if I can't be physically beside you, my heart will always find its way to you.

Four years, countless memories, a thousand reasons to stay, and one person I still want to spend my future with — YOU. Ikaw gihapon, bab. Ikaw ra gihapon akong pili-on. And if life gives us more years together, I hope it will still be you and me, growing, learning, loving, and choosing each other over and over again.

I love you always, bab. Happy 4th anniversary to us! Here's to our love, our dreams, and the day nga dili na ta LDR. Soon, we'll finally turn all our “I miss you” into “I'm here na.”
